# Player shell (R12 WP1, revised in R13 WP13 and WP18)

## Purpose

The full-screen player gives the artwork more of the phone, moves the heading one line lower, folds album browsing into the artist line, splits the bottom row into AirPlay (leading) and Lyrics plus Queue (trailing), and lets the right time label show the song length or the remaining time. R13 restores the like heart to the heading beside the title, runs the progress bar across the whole column, sizes the AirPlay glyph to the Lyrics and Queue symbols, and adds an upward swipe over the artwork or heading that opens a configurable sheet. `NowPlayingView` owns the shell; `PlayerArtistPickerSheet` owns the album and artists chooser; `PlayerLikeButton` owns the heart; `PlaybackControlGlyph` owns the play glyph shared with the mini-player; `PlayerProgressRow` owns the bar.

## Behaviour

- Artwork. Portrait phones keep the 24-point column for text and controls, and only the art extends 12 points beyond the column on each side (12-point side insets). The transport row is 80 points (play stays 80 by 80, previous and next 56 tall) and the bottom row is 44 points. Regular width caps the column at 660 points and the art at 600, and pads 40 points below the art. Measured (R13): iPhone 17 Pro 378 by 378 at x 12, iPhone SE 3 259 (the seek block is 8 points shorter than in R12, and the height-bound SE art absorbs it), iPad Pro 13 portrait 600.
- Heading. `HStack(alignment: .center, spacing: 4)` of the title and artist lines on the left and the 52 by 52 like button (`player.like`) on the right, followed by the play-count caption line. The heart is centred on the title plus artist block (28 plus 44 points at the default size), not on the caption; the title and artist texts give up the 56-point button slot and keep `lineLimit(1)`. The heading follows the art with the same 20-point padding on phones. Measured (17 Pro, window 402 by 874): art (12, 132, 378), title (24, 538, 97 by 24), artist line (24, 564, 298 by 44), heart (326, 546, 52 by 52), caption 608 to 624, heading gap below the art 28; SE 3 (375 by 667): art (58, 84, 259), title (24, 365), gap 22. The title stays below the window midline (437 and 333.5) on every device; it sits 8 points lower than in R12 because the seek block below it is 8 points shorter and the art region absorbs the difference.
- Like heart (`PlayerLikeButton`). Super liked: `bolt.heart.fill` in `AppTheme.accent`; liked: `heart.fill` in `AppTheme.accent`; unliked: `heart` in `AppTheme.artworkSecondaryText`; 22-point semibold symbol in a 52-point target. Labels "Like this song" and "Unlike this song", `.isSelected` while liked or super liked. A tap runs `MusicAppModel.toggleLike(_:surface: .player)`; a 0.4 s hold runs `toggleSuperLike(_:surface: .player)`. The glyph and colour flip without animation (`.transaction { $0.animation = nil }`); a like bounces the glyph with a light impact haptic, a promotion adds a scale bounce, a spark burst and the success haptic, and presses scale to 0.88 through `PlayerHeartButtonStyle`. Reduce Motion keeps the haptics and the colour/glyph changes and drops the bounces, burst and press animation. [PLAYER_HEART.md](PLAYER_HEART.md) owns the glyphs, hold, motion and colour checks.
- Play button. The 80 by 80 target draws `PlaybackControlGlyph`: `play.fill` or `pause.fill` at 28-point bold (the triangle nudged 2 points right) morph through `.contentTransition(.symbolEffect(.replace.downUp.byLayer))` in 150 ms; while loading the glyph pulses and one thin arc (70-point diameter, 3-point round-capped stroke in `AppTheme.background`, 30 percent of the circle) turns once per second inside the button, so the button keeps its shape and there is no spinner. Reduce Motion keeps the pulse and drops the morph and arc. The mini-player's 52-point button uses the same glyph at 20-point semibold with a 38-point, 2-point arc in `AppTheme.primaryText` and adds the retry state (`arrow.clockwise`) while playback has an error. Current-song badges (`waveform` in rows, Home cards and the Queue) animate with `.variableColor.iterative.dimInactiveLayers` while playing and hold still when paused or under Reduce Motion.
- Progress bar (`PlayerProgressRow`, `PlayerProgressBar`). One 3.5-point round-capped stroke spanning the column: the track in `artworkSecondaryText` at 35 percent (60 under Increase Contrast) with the accent progress on top, and the 10-point scrub marker while dragging. The row is 32 points tall in the layout and the seek target (`player.seek`, a Slider representation) is 44 points, overhanging the row by 6 points above and below; the time labels start where the target ends (`VStack(spacing: 6)`), so the visible distance from the bar to the time text stays 22 points. Touches map linearly across the whole width (`PlayerProgressBar.progress(atX:width:) = x / width`, clamped); the horizontal `PlayerSeekPanGesture` and the tap gesture are unchanged. No `zIndex` is needed any more: nothing overlaps the time row.
- Artist line (`player.artistName`). One credit and no album id navigates directly to the artist (label "Go to artist, <names>"). Any album id or more than one credit presents the album and artists chooser (`.browse(track)`, label "Album and artists, <names>"). The line is disabled only with no credits and no album. The chooser keeps its `player.browse.sheet`, `player.browse.list`, `player.browse.album`, `player.browse.done` and `player.artist.<index>` identifiers. The More sheet's own "Go to artist" route is unchanged.
- Bottom row. `HStack(spacing: 8) { AirPlay, Download, Spacer, Lyrics, Queue }` at 44 points. AirPlay is the native `AVRoutePickerView`, exactly 44 by 44 (`player.airplay.anchor`), at the column's leading edge, with the Download button 8 points after it; Lyrics (`player.lyrics`, `quote.bubble`) and Queue (`player.queue`, `text.line.first.and.arrowtriangle.forward`) are 20-point medium symbols in 44 by 44 targets at the trailing edge, Queue outermost, 8 points apart. AVKit draws its glyph as a bare 18 by 18 `CALayer` inside the inner `UIButton` (not a symbol image, so `preferredSymbolConfigurationForImage` has no effect), so `PlayerAirPlayTrigger.attach(to:)` sets the button layer's `sublayerTransform` to a 1.15 scale about its centre: every view frame, and so every accessibility frame the AirPlay suite asserts, stays exactly as before, and nothing is drawn over AVKit's glyph. Measured glyph boxes (non-backdrop pixels at 3x on the 17 Pro dark capture), before: AirPlay 18.0 by 17.7, Lyrics 22.0 by 20.7, Queue 26.7 by 19.0; after: AirPlay 20.7 by 20.3, Lyrics 22.0 by 20.7, Queue 26.7 by 19.0 (heights within 1.7 points; light 20.7 by 20.0; SE 3 and iPad at 2x: 21.0 by 20.5, 22.0 by 20.5, 26.5 by 19.0). The scaled layer stays crisp at 3x. Foreground colour, the 40 percent disabled opacity, the popover anchor and the dismissal-pan suspension while routes are presented are unchanged.
- Download button (`PlayerDownloadButton`, `player.download`, R13 WP18). A 44 by 44 target whose glyph follows `MusicAppModel.downloadState(for:)` for the current song, drawn at the Lyrics and Queue weight (20-point medium): not downloaded `arrow.down.circle` in `artworkSecondaryText` ("Download", tap enqueues); queued `arrow.down.circle.dotted` ("Download queued") or waiting for Wi-Fi ("Waiting for Wi-Fi"), tap cancels; downloading a 20-point ring (2-point track at 35 percent, accent arc from 12 o'clock, inset by half the stroke) around a 6-point stop square ("Downloading, 42 percent", value "42 percent", the same 99 percent cap as the More row), tap cancels; downloaded `arrow.down.circle.fill` in `AppTheme.accent` ("Downloaded"), tap opens the "Remove download?" confirmation (Remove download, `player.download.remove`, and Cancel) that demotes the song to the cache per [DOWNLOADS.md](DOWNLOADS.md); failed `exclamationmark.arrow.circlepath` ("Download failed, retry", tap retries through `download`). "Download unavailable" (disabled, glyph at 0.4 opacity): no current song, no download center, a Gaana song whose current stream is HLS (or not yet known), or offline with neither a local copy nor a pending item (offline promotion of a complete cached copy stays enabled, and pending items stay tappable so they can be cancelled). The removal dialog captures the song at tap time. State swaps do not animate; only the ring's fraction does (not under Reduce Motion). The button is outside `PlayerSwipeUpRegion`, and a tap needs no travel so it never starts the dismissal pan. The More sheet's Download row is unchanged and agrees with the button. Measured (17 Pro, 402 by 874): AirPlay (24, 790, 44, 44), Download (76, 790, 44, 44), Lyrics (282, 790), Queue (334, 790); glyph boxes on the dark capture at 3x: AirPlay 20.7 by 20.3, Download 20.3 by 20.3 (idle, filled and disabled alike), downloading ring 20.0 by 20.0, Lyrics 22.0 by 20.7, Queue 26.7 by 19.3.
- Swipe up (`PlayerSwipeUpRegion`). A `UIPanGestureRecognizer` for the artwork region and for the heading only (not the seek row, transport row, bottom row or top bar). Each region is a background `UIViewRepresentable` that never hit-tests itself; it installs the recognizer on the cover's hosting view and accepts only touches that begin inside its own bounds, so a swipe that starts on the heart or the artist line is seen before SwiftUI's button press claims the touch (a `.gesture()` representable on the heading would wait for the press to fail). It recognises simultaneously with the dismissal pan. It begins only for mostly vertical upward travel (`|dy| > 1.15 |dx|`, `dy < 0`) and acts when the finger lifts with `translation.y <= -60` or `velocity.y <= -900` (and `|dx| < |dy|`), presenting the sheet chosen by `UserPreferences.playerSwipeUpAction`: `.lyrics` (default) presents `.lyrics`, `.queue` presents `.queue`, `.moreOptions` presents `.actions(track, .options)`, `.off` does nothing. Disabled while a sheet, the AirPlay routes, a scrub or a dismissal is active. Taps on the heart and artist line still work because a pan needs travel to begin; a swipe that starts on the heart cancels the press. Downward travel belongs to the dismissal pan, a horizontal drag on the bar stays a seek, and the sheet is presented after the gesture ends so it never competes with the finger. Reduce Motion changes nothing.
- Settings > Playback groups sound, transitions, player actions and shuffle controls. "Swipe up on the player" (`settings.playerSwipeUp`, `hand.draw`) offers Lyrics, Queue, More options and Off. There is no separate Right time label setting; the timer tap persists `trailingTimeStyle`. [SETTINGS.md](SETTINGS.md) owns the page layout.
- Right time label (`player.trailingTime`). Unchanged from R12: a Button whose content follows `UserPreferences.trailingTimeStyle`; tapping flips the preference through `MusicAppModel.updatePreferences`. The 44-point target grows leftward and downward, never upward into the seek row.
- Accent at launch. `NowPlayingView` applies `.accentColor` and `.tint` with `model.library.preferences.accent.color` itself. The cover presented by `--player` at launch used to fall back to system blue because it could miss the root's accent; the fixture captures now render coral like the mini-player flow.
- Animated backdrop (`PlayerArtworkBackdrop`, `Sources/UI/PlayerBackgroundMotion.swift`). Settings > Appearance > Player Background (`settings.playerBackground`, `UserPreferences.playerBackgroundMotion`) offers Off (default), Drift, Pulse and React to Music; collection pages never animate. Off renders the shared `ArtworkBackdropSurface` unchanged. The other styles draw a 4 by 4 `MeshGradient` of the artwork's two colour families with no scrim or blurred cover over it, so the motion is plainly visible. Every tone sits in a luminance band chosen for the player's text (dark mode at most 0.12 relative luminance under white text; light mode at least 0.5 under near-black text), and unit tests check 4.5:1 contrast for every vertex and for blends between neighbours. A grey cover borrows the language or accent tint. Drift moves the mesh points (interior travel under half the grid spacing, so it never folds) and fades each vertex between the two colour families on a 19 s cycle at 24 fps, even while paused. Pulse breathes the colours between their quiet and resting tones on a 3.6 s cycle at 30 fps while playing and holds still while paused. React to Music maps the tap's level and onset (`PlaybackLevelMeter`) to colour energy and a short outward push of the mesh at 30 fps while playing; it falls back to Pulse (`reason=noTap`) when the current item has no tap: HLS (Gaana), live radio, unsupported PCM or a foreign mix. Every style is static (zero frames, the Off surface) under Reduce Motion, Reduce Transparency or Increase Contrast (flat tint), Low Power Mode and serious or critical thermal state, and pauses its TimelineView whenever the player is off screen, the scene is inactive or a player sheet is presented. Without artwork the mesh uses the existing `artworkTint`. Settings explains a held-still background (`settings.playerBackground.hold`: Reduce Motion, Reduce Transparency or Increase Contrast, Low Power Mode or a warm device), so a static player never looks like a broken setting.
- Artwork exposes a `player.artwork` accessibility container (no label, no VoiceOver stop) so layout checks can measure it and swipe on it.

## Metrics

| Metric or event fields | Emitted when | Dimensions / bounded values | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `event=trailing_time_toggled style=<songLength|remaining>` (OSLog, subsystem `com.agraabhi.ganabajana`, category `Player`, info) | The right time label is tapped and the preference flips | `style` is the new `TrailingTimeStyle.rawValue`, two values | Shows whether the tap-to-switch is used and which style listeners settle on |
| `event=swipe_up action=<lyrics|queue|moreOptions|off>` (same logger, info) | An upward swipe over the artwork or heading qualifies, before the sheet is presented; `off` logs and does nothing | `action` is `PlayerSwipeUpAction.rawValue`, four values | Shows whether the gesture is discovered and which sheet listeners route it to; no track or position data |

| `event=player_download_tap state=<idle|queued|waitingForWiFi|downloading|downloaded|failed|removeConfirmed>` (same logger, info; R13 WP18) | The bottom-row Download button is tapped while enabled, and again when the removal is confirmed | `state` is the state tapped from, seven values; no track ID | Shows whether the bottom-row control is used and for which action (download, cancel, retry, remove); transfer outcomes stay in the `Downloads` category |

| `event=player_background style=<off|drift|pulse|reactive> effective=<static|drift|pulse|reactive> reason=<none|reduceMotion|reduceTransparency|lowPower|thermal|noTap>` (same logger, info) | The player backdrop's style, effect or fallback reason changes; deduplicated, never per frame | Three closed enums | Shows which styles are chosen and how often accessibility, power, heat or a missing tap override them |

No other metrics change. Preference persistence and sync errors surface through the existing `updatePreferences` path and library error handling. The heart's tap and hold go through `MusicAppModel.toggleLike(_:surface: .player)` and `toggleSuperLike(_:surface: .player)`, which log `event=super_like` ([SUPER_LIKES.md](SUPER_LIKES.md#metrics)); the shell adds nothing of its own.

## Not verified

- Animated backdrop: simulator captures only (`Round2-Background-UI-01`); no frame-time, thermal or battery measurement on a device; React to Music during Crossfade reads whichever deck published last; a measuring tap adds the documented tap priming delay to gapless swaps while the player is visible.

- Download button (WP18): no fixture song streams Gaana HLS (the Gaana fixture plays the file waveform), so the HLS "Download unavailable" branch is covered by code reading only; the offline, queued, waiting-for-Wi-Fi and failed glyphs were not driven by a UI test; the downloading capture shows the ring at 0 percent.

- The U+2212 glyph in the remaining style is visible only in screenshots; XCUITest reads the accessibility value, which stays the plain `m:ss`.
- `GanaBajanaPlayerProgressBarUITests.testBarSpansTheColumnAndTheHeartInTheHeadingStillLikes` cannot run on the iPad because the shared driver waits for the bottom tab bar (pre-existing; iPad exposes top `tab.*` buttons). The two direct-launch appearance tests cover the iPad, including the landscape branch.
- iPad landscape captures come from the headless scene-geometry request and are rotated in the attachment (known harness limitation).
- The store screenshot suite (`GanaBajanaStoreScreenshotsUITests`) is live-catalog only and was not run.
- Physical devices, VoiceOver traversal order, the native AirPlay route chooser (`testNativeAirPlayChooserReturnsToTheSamePlayingSong`, skipped without its runner flag) and the AirPlay glyph in the active-route state were not exercised.
