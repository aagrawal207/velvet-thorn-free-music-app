# Velvet Thorn

Native iPhone, iPad and Mac app (Mac Catalyst with the Mac idiom, same bundle ID; FFmpegBridge ships Catalyst slices) using Swift 6, SwiftUI, AVPlayer, and iOS 18+, with a watchOS 11+ companion and native CarPlay audio integration. App name: **Velvet Thorn** (owner's choice, 2 October 2026; Home Screen, in-app copy, widgets, Watch, CarPlay and intents). The App Store Connect record `6815025307` is still named `Breezy - Stream Music & Chill`; renaming it, its IAP localizations and the public Support/Privacy pages needs separate authorization. Widget and Control kinds (`Breezy.*`), intent type names, `BreezyWidgets`, the `Breezy/1.0` provider User-Agent, the extractor signing domain and cache paths are technical identities and keep their names.
